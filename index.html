What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small, strong posts placed into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. They provide a stable base for a dental crown, bridge, or denture.

Unlike removable teeth, implants are fixed in place and are designed to look and feel more like natural teeth. They can help improve chewing, speaking, and the appearance of your smile.

Dental implants are placed in stages. After the implant is placed, the jawbone needs time to heal and bond with it. Once it is stable, a custom-made crown or other restoration is attached to complete the new tooth.

Before treatment, your dentist will check your gums, jawbone, and overall oral health to determine whether this treatment is suitable for you.
